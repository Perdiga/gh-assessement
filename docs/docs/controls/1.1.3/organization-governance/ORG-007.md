# ORG-007: Outside collaborators have business justification

**Catalog version:** 1.1.3  
**Domain:** Organization Governance  
**Severity:** high  
**Maturity stage:** Mature  
**Well-Architected mapping:** Observability, Simplicity

## Objective

Establish and verify the organizational practice: outside collaborators have business justification.

## Requirement

Outside collaborators have business justification.

## Risk

Significant governance, security or operational exposure.

## Evidence sources

- **Source:** `Manual evidence / HR or IAM source`
  - **Fields:** `business_justification`
  - **Evidence key:** `outside_collaborators.justification`

## Evaluation logic

**Mode:** `manual_or_hybrid`

Evaluate collected evidence for `business_justification` against the stated requirement; distinguish missing evidence from a failing control.

## Expected result

PASS when the requirement is demonstrably satisfied; PARTIAL when implementation is incomplete; FAIL when materially absent; NOT ASSESSED when evidence is unavailable; N/A only with documented rationale.

## Exception criteria

A documented, approved exception with owner, rationale, compensating control and expiry.

## Remediation guidance

Remediate the identified gap for: Outside collaborators have business justification. Assign an accountable owner and target date.

## Maturity-stage basis

Organizationally established, standardized or governed practice aligned to the GitHub Well-Architected Mature stage.
