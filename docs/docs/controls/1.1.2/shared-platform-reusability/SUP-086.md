# SUP-086: Shared workflows are security-reviewed

**Catalog version:** 1.1.2  
**Domain:** Shared Platform & Reusability  
**Severity:** critical  
**Maturity stage:** Mature  
**Well-Architected mapping:** Scalability, Resiliency, Efficiency, Disaster Recovery, Modularity, Interoperability, Simplicity, Observability

## Objective

Establish and verify the organizational practice: shared workflows are security-reviewed.

## Requirement

Shared workflows are security-reviewed.

## Risk

Material security, availability or compliance exposure.

- **Source:** `PR/ruleset evidence`

  - **Evidence key:** `sup.security_review`

Remediate the identified gap for: Shared workflows are security-reviewed. Assign an accountable owner and target date.

Organizationally established, standardized or governed practice aligned to the GitHub Well-Architected Mature stage.
